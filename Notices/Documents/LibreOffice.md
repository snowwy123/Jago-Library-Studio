# LibreOffice document renderer

The complete development trial includes an unmodified LibreOffice 26.8.1.1 Windows x64 administrative image in Tools/Documents/LibreOffice. It is called as a separate process for document-to-PDF conversion. A smaller or source build can connect an installed LibreOffice instead.

The renderer retains its original license.txt, LICENSE.html, NOTICE, readmes and bundled component notices. LibreOffice source is MPL-2.0; individual third-party components retain their accompanying licences. Jago has not modified these binaries.
